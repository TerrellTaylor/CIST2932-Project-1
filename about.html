About Me

My name is Terrell Taylor. I'm 20 years old and in college for a computer programming associate's degree. My plan is to leave college and use the degree to get into an entry-level programming position, where I will work my way up the corporate ladder while gaining work experience that can be used anywhere. My end goal is to work for a game studio, like Infinity Ward, but I wouldn't complain if I made good money elsewhere.

Work aside, I am a very simple person on the outside. Gaming is my main hobby and what I am normally occupying my time with. I am also Christian, so I read my Bible and use lessons from it in my life, even when I stumble. I enjoy gaming with my friends online, but I wish they could be in my area again so I have a reason to go to and hang out at places that aren't only about necessity or are boring when alone.
